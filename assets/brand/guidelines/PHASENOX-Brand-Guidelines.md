# PHASENØX Brand Guidelines

## Core identity
PHASENØX is dark, cinematic, technical and restrained. It belongs to audio intelligence, perception, reasoning and agentic tooling. The system should feel unsettling and precise rather than colorful or sci-fi decorative.

## Wordmark
Public wordmark: **PHASENØX**. Canonical ASCII: **PHASENOX**. The Ø is replaced by the eye glyph in primary artwork.

## Eye glyph
The eye is the O/Ø — never a mascot placed beside the name. It is monochrome, angular and minimal. The pupil contains a tiny waveform; the diagonal slash preserves the Ø silhouette and suggests phase. Red is allowed only as a tiny signal/accent inside the eye or live-state assets.

## Visual language
- Near-black backgrounds.
- Bone-white typography.
- Sparse waveform / spectrum / phase traces.
- Subtle technical grids only where functional.
- Cinematic tension through negative space, scale and contrast — not glow.
- No purple, neon rainbow, glossy 3D eye, merchandise mockups, cyberpunk decoration or generic AI-brain imagery.

## Product relevance
Visual motifs may reference waveform, spectrum, phase, signal/noise, auditory perception, reasoning graphs and agent execution.

## Tagline
**HEAR BEYOND THE NOISE**


## LOCKED WORDMARK REFINEMENT
- Final public wordmark: **PHASENØX**.
- Primary display rhythm: **PHASE   NØX**. PHASE and NØX are deliberately separated by negative space; NØX remains a tight terminal cluster.
- Compact lockups may close the PHASE/NØX gap only when available width requires it.
- The eye/Ø concept is unchanged. The complete iris/cornea, waveform and diagonal phase slash are clipped inside the outer eye silhouette: no internal circular or diagonal geometry may protrude beyond the eye frame.
- Preserve the dark monochrome cinematic system and restrained signal-red accent only.
